### 1. [WHMCS SQL 注入漏洞可免登入竊取客戶資料與管理員密碼雜湊 (WHMCS SQL Injection Could Expose Customer Data and Admin Password Hashes without Login)](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/whmcs-sql-injection-flaw-could-expose.html)
