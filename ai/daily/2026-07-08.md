### 2. [Image2Sim：用生成式神經模擬器擴大 embodied navigation (Image2Sim: Scaling Embodied Navigation via Generative Neural Simulator)](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.05765)

* **相關標籤：** `#WorldModels` `#OpenSource` `#Interaction`
