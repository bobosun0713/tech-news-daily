### 1. [LLM 供應商切換不需停機 (How to Switch LLM Providers Without Downtime)](https://konghq.com/blog/enterprise/how-to-switch-llm-providers-without-downtime)

* **相關標籤：** `#LLM` `#Reliability` `#MLOps`

### 2. [在持久化 agent 工作流中做 token 計帳與取消 (Token accounting and cancellation in durable agent workflows)](https://agentbayes.com/blog/stopping-a-streaming-llm-agent)
* **核心重點：** 針對流式 LLM 與 agent 任務補上成本可視化、取消控制與工作流中止機制。
* **相關標籤：** `#Agents` `#Streaming` `#CostControl`

* **相關標籤：** `#Benchmark` `#Agents` `#Evaluation`
